01 · Scouting · CEBL

Playoff Advanced Scouting

Project overview

I was tasked with creating a "Target Time Report" on our semi-finals matchup. In this report I analyzed the Niagara River Lions 10 most recent games tracking every possession, detailing key contributors, most common sets and PNR combinations, and primary means of scoring. This report was used by the coaching staff to help influence our late game strategy which ultimately led to a CEBL championship game appearance.

In these edits I would detail strengths and weaknesses alongside associated clips to help give our managers/coaches an efficient shallow overview of the players.

03 · Analytics · CEBL

Game-Day Stat Tracking

Depending on the needs of our coaches I would manually track various statistical categories during games, including opponent PNR PPP, opponent 3pt generation and results, PPP against our various PNR coverages, key opponents shot attempts and results.

These stats were aggregated into charts and used by the coaching staff for mid game adjustments and post-game breakdowns.
